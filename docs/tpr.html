Profit in dollars vs. the price reaction

Each quarter sits at the height of its report-day price move. The hollow circle ○ is the profit per share analysts expected; the filled dot ● is what actually came out. The dashed line marks what analysts expect at the next report. One caution: for many retailers the December (holiday) quarter is naturally the year's biggest, so dollar profits are best compared with the same quarter a year earlier.

Beating expectations (in %) vs. what the price did

Each dot is one report: how far profits came in above or below the analyst estimate (left–right), and what the share price did on report day (up–down). Dots in the lower-right corner beat expectations and the price still fell.

Quarter by quarter

Every change is measured against the report: "before" columns show how much the price rose or fell over that stretch leading into the report (ending at the last close before results); the highlighted report day column is the move on the day results came out; "after" columns show what happened from the report-day close onward. Weeks are trading weeks (5 market days).
